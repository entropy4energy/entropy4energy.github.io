Joining the S4E Lab

PhD students. Current Johns Hopkins students: email the S4E team (s4e-admissions AT jhu.edu) with your CV, an unofficial transcript, and a short paragraph on which research direction interests you and any relevant coursework or programming experience, and we will schedule a meeting. External applicants: submit an application to the Department of Materials Science and Engineering and name Prof. Oses as a faculty advisor of interest. Applications are due October 15 for spring admission and December 15 for fall admission. Questions about the application process go to Mrs. Lauren Rodgers (lmodica1 AT jhu.edu).

Postdoctoral researchers. Apply through the Interfolio link in the relevant open postings below. The cover letter must address each qualification explicitly. Questions go to the S4E team (s4e-admissions AT jhu.edu) with "S4E Post-Doc" in the subject line.

Undergraduate and master’s students. Current Johns Hopkins students: email the S4E team (s4e-admissions AT jhu.edu) with your CV, an unofficial transcript, a short paragraph on which research direction interests you, and when you would like to start. Positions are for research credit or a thesis project, not for pay. Summer positions for external undergraduates are posted below when a program is open.
