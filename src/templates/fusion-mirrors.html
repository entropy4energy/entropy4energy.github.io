Methods and scope

What is calculated?

VASP independent-particle PBE dielectric responses. Standard and GW-oriented PAW potentials are two potential choices within PBE, not a comparison of PBE and quasiparticle GW. Density and current responses are kept separate. Normal-z optical modes and reflectivity use the full tensor; Cartesian n/k are diagonal diagnostics. Mode labels are sorted per wavelength, not eigenvector-tracked through crossings.

Coverage, validation and experimental use

Only finite, passive n/k and valid reflected powers are shown. No extrapolation, zero-filling or band averaging is performed. Near-IR denotes this study’s restricted 700–1,200 nm region. Family labels follow the supplied archive. Source CSVs retain the full tensor and quality flags, including unplotted rows.

Use the explorer to examine spectral coverage and compare optical response at a diagnostic wavelength. These are bare, ideal bulk surfaces, not measured device performance. The data do not determine coating noise, roughness, damage thresholds, plasma erosion or fusion lifetime. In particular, bare-surface reflectivity is not a qualification of a LIGO interferometer mirror.

Private review and future public release

This review contains website code only. Opening a local folder does not publish it. The explorer has no upload API, remote-data endpoint or persistent data storage. Reloading the page clears its selection. Selected local file contents are not sent to the group’s website analytics.

Public data hosting, licensing, a citation and a versioned mirror address must be approved separately before release. The existing DFT reproduction package remains separate from the site code. Exported figures and CSVs contain the selected results: share them only with authorized reviewers.
