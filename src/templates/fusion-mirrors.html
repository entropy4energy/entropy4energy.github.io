Optical response

Reflectivity R is the percentage of incident light reflected. These calculations describe the optical response of ideal, uncoated materials.

Optical definitions

n and k denote the normal-z mode 1 refractive index and extinction coefficient. R is the unpolarized full-tensor reflectivity with Cartesian z as the surface normal. Gaps indicate unsupported or invalid values.

Calculation details

Density functional theory (DFT) optical responses were calculated with VASP. The spectra use the 1 nm wavelength grid from the screening calculations.

Standard and GW-oriented projector-augmented-wave (PAW) datasets both use the Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) functional. The GW label identifies a PAW dataset choice within PBE.

The spectra describe ideal, uncoated material surfaces at normal incidence.
