Synthesizability Game

A high-entropy ceramic for nuclear waste holds several elements on each of its two cation sites. Some of these mixtures can be made in the laboratory and some cannot. Find a combination of elements that can be made.

  1. Pick the A site or the B site, then tap elements in the periodic table to add them. Elements can also be dragged onto a site.
  2. Press Check to see the synthesizability score, from 0 to 100.
  3. A score of 50 or more means the composition can be made. Below 50, change the elements and check again.
