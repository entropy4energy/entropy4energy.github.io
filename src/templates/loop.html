Contents

Experiments

Synthesis recipes and the trials made with them, with X-ray diffraction patterns and Rietveld refinements.

Literature

Results reported in publications, attached to the recipes they describe, with their DOI.

Calculations

Density functional theory (DFT) results, attached to the materials they describe.

Predictions

Candidate high-entropy oxides of five 3d transition metals, ranked by the concerns flagged for their elements and processing, then by formability.

Access

LOOP is open to members of the Entropy for Energy Laboratory at Johns Hopkins University and its partners, with an approved account. Create an account and confirm your email address from the message you receive; the S4E Laboratory then approves the account. If you already have an account, sign in.

Records are shared by organization. Laboratory accounts see every record, and an account from a partner organization sees the records shared with its organization.

API access

The REST API returns the records your account can see, as JSON. The API documentation is open to everyone. To use the API, sign in, create a key under API keys and send it in the X-API-Key header.
