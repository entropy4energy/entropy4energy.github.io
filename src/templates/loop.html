LOOP

High-entropy materials from the laboratory, the literature and computation, in one place.

LOOP is the Learning and Optimization Platform. It brings together synthesis recipes, experiments and published results so you can see what has been made, how it was made and what happened. First-principles calculations from CHAOS add the computational side.

What's in LOOP

Experiments

See how materials were synthesized and what happened when they were made. Experimental records can include X-ray diffraction patterns and Rietveld refinements.

Literature

Find high-entropy materials and synthesis results reported in published papers.

Calculations

Material pages include density functional theory (DFT) calculations from CHAOS, including calculated structures and properties.

Predictions

Explore high-entropy oxide candidates selected for future experiments.

Access
